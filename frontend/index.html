From Floor Plan to 3D Model in 4 Steps

Our AI-powered process makes it simple. No technical skills needed.

1

Upload Your Floor Plan

Drop your JPG, PNG, or PDF floor plan into BuildView 3D. We accept any format from any architect.

2

AI Analyses It

Our AI detects every room, wall, door, and window automatically. If the scale is missing, we ask for one known measurement.

3

3D Model Generated

A fully interactive 3D house is built from your floor plan in minutes, with 2.7m ceiling heights and metric dimensions.

Explore & Customise
